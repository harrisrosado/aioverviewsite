Pillar one

Understand your work

Before your team can act, they need context. These AI capabilities surface insights, answer questions, and capture knowledge automatically.

Sidekick

Your AI-powered work companion

Sidekick lives inside the monday.com interface and understands the full context of your boards, items, updates, and documents. Ask it anything — from "what's blocking this project?" to "summarize last week's progress on the marketing launch."

Answers questions about your data in natural language
Generates status updates, summaries, and action items
Available everywhere — boards, items, docs, and dashboards

Demo moment

"Sidekick, what are the top 3 risks across all active projects?"

monday AI Notetaker

Meetings become actionable, automatically

The AI Notetaker joins your meetings, transcribes conversations, and pushes structured outcomes directly into monday.com. Decisions become board updates. Action items become tasks. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Auto-joins and records Zoom, Teams, and Meet calls
Generates structured summaries, action items, and key decisions
Creates and assigns tasks on your boards directly from conversations

Demo moment

"Here's a meeting from this morning — watch how the action items landed as tasks."

Pillar two

Create with AI

These tools put generative AI directly into your workspace — so anyone on the team can produce content, build formulas, and even spin up entire applications without writing code.

AI Blocks

AI-powered columns and widgets

AI Blocks are modular AI capabilities that live inside your boards as columns and dashboard widgets. They perform tasks like categorization, sentiment analysis, text generation, and formula writing — row by row, automatically.

Generate text, extract data, and categorize items inline
Write formulas in plain English — AI translates to monday syntax
Dashboard AI widgets for instant summaries and trend analysis

Demo moment

"Watch this AI column auto-categorize 50 support tickets the moment they arrive."

monday Vibe

Build apps by describing what you need

Vibe coding on monday.com lets anyone create custom applications, dashboards, and internal tools — just by describing what they want in plain language. The AI generates the app, connects it to your monday data, and deploys it inside your workspace.

Describe an app in natural language and watch it materialize
Connects natively to monday boards, items, and data
No developers required — operations, marketing, and HR teams can self-serve

Demo moment

"Let me build a client-facing portal in 60 seconds, live."

Pillar three

Automate the work

The real power of AI is when it moves beyond answering questions and starts doing the work. These capabilities handle processes, make decisions, and take action inside your workflows.

AI Workflows

Smart automations with AI steps

monday's automation builder now includes AI-powered steps. Instead of just moving items and sending notifications, your workflows can now analyze content, generate responses, categorize data, and make context-aware routing decisions.

Add AI steps to any existing automation recipe
Analyze, summarize, and route items based on content — not just fields
Combine with integrations to trigger cross-platform actions intelligently

Demo moment

"When a lead comes in, AI reads the description, scores it, assigns it to the right rep, and drafts the first reply."

AI Agents

Autonomous digital workers on your team

AI Agents go beyond single-step automations. They're goal-oriented digital workers that can own multi-step processes end to end — monitoring boards, making decisions, escalating issues, and collaborating with your team through updates and notifications.

Define a goal and let the agent figure out the steps
Built with monday's Agent Factory — visual, no-code agent creation
Agents can read boards, write updates, change statuses, and trigger other automations

Demo moment

"This agent monitors overdue items every morning, pings owners, and escalates to leadership if nothing moves in 48 hours."
